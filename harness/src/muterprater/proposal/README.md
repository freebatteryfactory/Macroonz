# Proposal

This home owns the semantic vocabulary and invariant nucleus for turning observed openings into reviewable proposals and for the explicit human-admission operation over a reviewed proposal.

It owns survivor explanations, gaps and sketches, owed claims and declarations, proof shapes and obligation lanes, inferred obligations, discharge evidence and demonstrations, proof deltas and comparisons, proposal grounds and destinations, proposal documents and their replay-bearing subset, the three proposal forms, proposal refusals, stored proposal references, the proposal sink, and human-admission receipts and refusals.

The `propose` operation module carries the existing public verb road from synthesis through explicit human admission.

The parent keeps that verb path while this owner home remains private.

Descriptor owns proposal and replay identities, origins, admission grounds, row vocabulary, and canonical row bytes.

Depot capsules owns replay entries, replay storage, stored replay references, and storage effects.

Verdict owns mutation outcomes, targets, reports, and intended rejection.

The frozen parent `wrap.rs` supplies mutant-scoped execution without moving into this home.

A proposal sink reports caller-owned review custody, and a replay depot reports caller-owned replay custody.

Neither seam proves filesystem durability, creates canonical identity, or admits a proposal without the explicit human operation.
