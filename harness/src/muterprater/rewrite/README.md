# rewrite

Rewrite owns structural mutation descriptors, their refusal-ordered guards, declared descriptor rosters, audit candidates, admission posture, and the artifact-mutation seed vocabulary.

A descriptor is data rather than an executable mutation, and planning preserves the roster's declared order while placing every candidate under audit-pending trust.

`admission_for` uses interpretation's selection admission to check a pair and exact selection without invoking callbacks or requiring a prior kill.
`admission` projects the stricter compiled-rejection availability composition.
Both leave descriptors audit-pending.
Rewrite consumes verdict-owned operator-family references and pressure-owned scope shapes through the parent compatibility surface.

`ArtifactMutation` and `ARTIFACT_MUTATIONS` are seed vocabulary rather than an execution lane or an evidence owner.
The surgery that realizes a seed and the observer that judges it remain with their own owners.

This home derives no identity, emits no encoded bytes, executes no rewrite, and mints no evidence.
Admission says only that the interpreted audit road is available; actual execution must earn any later evidence.
