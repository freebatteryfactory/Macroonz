# interpretation

Interpretation owns production and evaluation call types and bindings, observations, evaluation pairs and their standing, mutation witnesses, no-mutation results and parity, interpreted trust and availability, interpreted mutation evidence, execution refusals, and the substrate names those values require.

The private noun home and the public verb are distinct: this home defines no public `muterprater::interpretation` ontology.
Its values remain on the existing `muterprater::*` surface, while `muterprater::interpret` is the narrow public operation over them.
The [archive](archive/README.md), exposed as `muterprater::interpretation_archive`, retains complete historical no-mutation readings and interpreted evidence through explicit typed caller encodings.
Loaded records preserve their historical authority ceiling and cannot become current qualification or trust.

## Scoped observation and assessment

The [public mutation assessment example](../../../../examples/mutation_assessment/README.md) composes this road with actual compilation, independently authored weak and strong checks, historical storage, and an unadmitted proposal whose saved result reaches current-binding replay.

`interpret::observe_mutation` resolves the pair and exact selected membership before calling production or evaluation.
It retains production and both evaluation outcomes, including call refusals.
The specimen observer consumes that reading and retains exact source and both compiled outcomes without consulting a witness.
`qualify_execution` borrows those complete observations and requires the three corresponding-road comparisons, zero unchanged firings and positive selected firings.
Its shared-substrate declaration states the comparison's independence ceiling.

`observe_witness` checks the invocation profile, target and point's owner claim before running the supplied witness against each retained meaning.
Reports record that actual judgment and its measurement, not another compilation or subject execution.
`qualify_witness` requires all unchanged judgments to pass and the two selected judgments to agree, retaining the entire reading beside any refusal.
The resulting `MutationAssessment` joins the original selection and firing count to the actual selected witness trial.
An owner-relation difference between production and selected meaning earns scoped `EquivalenceAxis::Refuted`; agreement leaves equivalence `NotAssessed`.

The input remains a borrowed generic Rust value under a unit invocation.
It has no input-aware execution-key coordinate or immutable-snapshot guarantee.
Saved witness execution composes the [input owner](../../input/README.md) and the [runner](../../runner/README.md#saved-witness-replay) with explicitly encoded results and current caller bindings.
Interior mutation, unstable callbacks and coarse equivalence remain caller ceilings; agreement of corresponding actual witness conclusions is checked separately from the equivalence relation.

## Dependencies

Interpretation consumes discovery's surfaces, directives, selections, and refusal vocabulary through the parent `muterprater` paths.
It consumes backend suite pressure and verdict evidence through those same parent paths, and consumes specimen's compiled projection pressure without absorbing specimen custody or host semantics.
The ordinary observation road shares passive pair and selection data without either home requiring the other's prior judgment.
The stricter compiled-rejection composition additionally retains no-mutation qualification, generic suite pressure and exact projection rejection.
Its `InterpretedMutationEvidence` does not retain compiled meanings or establish selected-road correspondence; that stronger contract belongs to `MutationAssessment`.
Trial bindings and reports remain owned by `runner` and `report`, and equivalence and substrate vocabulary remain owned by `properties`.

## Nonclaims

A function pointer remains a caller statement, and interpretation records only the values, firings, reports, and joins its typed operations observe.
Generic suite pressure, compiled projection pressure, and interpreted activation remain distinct evidence routes.
No interpretation value discovers a mutation, invokes a compiler host, or changes verdict or backend semantics.
