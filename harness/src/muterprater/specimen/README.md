# specimen

Specimen owns exact compiled pressure over one separately materialized, production-shaped selected projection.

This home owns compiler-source content identity, materializer and host bindings, compiled specimen requests and observations, and the standing and refusals of exact projection pressure.
The public `muterprater::specimen` module is its operation door, while its types remain available through the parent `muterprater` type surface rather than a public child ontology.

Specimen consumes discovery's surface, selection, and directive vocabulary, interpretation's pair, parity, and witness vocabulary, and verdict's mutation vocabulary through their parent compatibility paths.
Interpretation consumes compiled projection pressure in return, so the two homes keep distinct ownership across a reference cycle.

The operation binds bytes-only artifact identity to the exact pair, selection, execution, check, and reports produced through a caller-owned compiler host.
It records what the host reports and does not claim to observe the host's process.
Wrapped-backend pressure is a separate evidence book and cannot substitute for exact compiled specimen pressure.
