# archive

Owned historical compiled projection pressure through specimen's existing content and execution boundaries.

`retain_projection` retains the complete qualified parity, exact unchanged and selected source bytes, selected standing, both compiled trial reports and the complete mutation report.
It invokes the declared input encoder once and the meaning encoder once for each parity meaning reached.
It invokes no materializer, compiler host or witness.
The compiled meanings themselves were consumed by the live operation and are not reconstructed.
`read_projection` owns its complete data without callbacks or conversions into live pressure, parity qualification, selection or execution.

## Consistency and authority

Artifact identities are rederived under specimen's existing bytes-only content domain.
Empty and non-UTF-8 source buffers are lawful, but their identities must differ.
The selected standing shares its artifact with the selected content and its pair, execution and check with qualified parity.
Retention checks those live relationships before sharing their wire representation.
The historical standing projects those same admitted fields; it has no independent contradictory labels.

Both compiled reports must reproduce parity's execution key and replay posture.
Their sites must equal each other but may differ from the parity reports' site.
The unchanged trial must pass and the selected trial must refuse.
The mutation must carry the compiled-projection selection, a declared family and activation site, the witness's owner claim, and the selected trial's entire demonstrated finding.
Its axes must match the live compiled-projection road.
The standalone record has no full surface preimage and cannot establish point membership, operation bytes or family/site correspondence beyond those retained claims.

Internal consistency and envelope integrity establish neither authenticity nor current execution.
Retaining caller-encoded values does not prove an earlier interior-state snapshot.
Current compiler-host evidence remains a separate caller obligation.

## Format and bounds

All integers are big-endian and framed bytes have an eight-byte length prefix.
The envelope starts with the thirty-two-byte body address under `PROJECTION_ARCHIVE_TAG`.
Its body contains format `u32(1)`, kind `u32(1)`, historical custody `u32(0)`, framed qualified-parity archive, unchanged artifact, selected artifact, historical selection, framed unchanged trial, framed selected trial and framed mutation report.
Each artifact is its framed thirty-two-byte bytes-only identity followed by its framed exact content.
The [discovery archive](../../discovery/archive/README.md) owns the selection member grammar.
Nested parity, trial and mutation archives keep their existing formats.

`ProjectionArchiveLimits` independently bounds outer envelope and fields, nested parity, each compiled trial, the mutation report and each source buffer.
Retention preflights all known metadata, nested bounds and minimum envelope size before any encoder callback.
Returned value bytes then face parity's existing limits and the complete projection envelope and field limits before envelope allocation.
Loading bounds the envelope before hashing and each field before copying.
Malformed slots, lengths, nested records, identities, joins and trailing material refuse.
