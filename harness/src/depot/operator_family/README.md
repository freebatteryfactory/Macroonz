# operator family

Mutation pressure needs a stable vocabulary for the generic kinds of damage it can attribute without inventing subject meaning.

This home owns that vocabulary.
Each authored family carries a stable slug and a concise account of what the damage attacks, while the complete bank is the authority against which mutation targets and rewrite plans resolve family membership.

The slug crosses identity-bearing mutation surfaces, so changing it is an identity decision rather than a documentation edit.
The account of what a family attacks is caller-facing meaning, but it does not enter those preimages.

## Evidence ceiling

Membership says that the harness can name a kind of damage.
It does not select a mutation point, apply a rewrite, establish activation, or decide what a surviving mutant means for a product.
Those operations remain with mutation pressure and the subject owner.
