# depot

Some facts are not measured, they are declared.

The harness inflicts a fixed set of damages on a subject, and it keeps a fixed set of its own types from standing in for one another.
Both are decisions somebody made once and wrote down, and this is where they are written down: plain `const` data under one owner, read by whichever instrument needs it.

Everything banked here is a fact about the harness itself.
Whoever adopts the harness keeps its own banks — its golden vectors, its populations, its own taxonomy — in its own tree, and hands them in as ordinary declared input.

## The banks

**`operator_families.rs`** — the damages a mutation may inflict.
A row is a stable slug and one sentence naming what an operator of that family attacks.
Choosing a point, applying a damage, and scoring what survived belong to the mutation home, `muterprater`; reading this bank runs nothing.

**`swap_pairs.rs`** — pairs of the harness's own types that must never be accepted for one another.
A row is material for a compile-refusal generator: offer the substitute where the seat's type is required, and the evidence is `rustc`'s refusal.
No count-the-rows lane pretends to be that generator.

**`capsules/`** — the entry a human admission stores when it carries a replay capsule, and the caller-owned trait that stores it.
It banks no rows of its own; it declares a shape and hands the storage out.

## How a family earns a seat

By naming a defect class that hides.

A damage is worth inflicting when a subject can suffer it and still pass the suite watching it: a comparison nudged off its edge, a refusal thrown away one line after it was returned, a host fact read where a declared input was handed over.
A damage any honest row would catch teaches nobody anything.

## What a survivor means

A survivor is the finding, never the score.

What a surviving mutant of a family means for the suite that let it live is written at that family's row, because that sentence is what an explanation gets authored against.
How many survived is not one of the facts this bank holds.

## What this home refuses to grow

It runs nothing, and nothing runs back into it.
A bank entry is authored, a run produces evidence, and evidence never writes a bank — a proposal is not an admission.

It holds nothing somebody else owns.
A fact lives in the depot of the package that owns it, and a product's facts stay with the product.
