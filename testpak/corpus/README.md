# corpus — seed-packs for warm-start fuzzing

One compressed seed-pack per subject: length-prefixed binary, no parser
dependency, machine-written and machine-read. A pack warm-starts the fuzz
lane's search; it holds nothing durable — a find that matters rides the
proposal road into an authored descriptor row, and that row is the record.
Packs are exploration state, not specification and not evidence. A pack
names its content by address, so corruption and duplication are
self-evident. A seed pack is never a judgment population: what judges is
generated from declared law or admitted by proposal — nothing is ever
judged against the corpus it searched with.
