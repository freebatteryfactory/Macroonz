# Network transcript presentation

This home projects the [network owner's](../../../harness/src/network/README.md) admitted transcript and separately earned reproduction and playback records.
The transcript retains its topology, source claim, optional complete simulation manifest, ordered deliveries and exact envelope.
Dropped sends and empty advances remain in the manifest even when they add no delivery row.
The display neither encodes payloads anew nor reconstructs a simulation census that the pack does not retain.

A simulated source claim does not establish reproduction, and a recorded-live claim does not authenticate live traffic.
Simulation reproduction, playback exhaustion and their same-address join have separate projections and retain their own counts and logical ticks.
Exhaustion establishes that rows were handed out; it says nothing about their processing by an adopter.
No projection advances playback, runs a simulation, joins evidence or admits a pack.
Transcript refusals retain their typed cause without guessing which reader, writer or reproduction operation the caller invoked.
