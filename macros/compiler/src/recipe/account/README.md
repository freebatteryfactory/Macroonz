# account

These files are the nucleus children of the recipe declarations: each is declared from `types.rs` with `#[path]` so it reaches the private fields no sibling module can, and the directory has no door of its own.

- `informed.rs` constructs one informed recipe from structural parts and answers every readback question about it.
- `relation.rs` constructs one informed relation from authored rows and the vocabularies its endpoints name.
- `consuming.rs` admits explicit resource, runtime, payload and method bindings against the selected transition relation.
- `edit.rs` re-declares an explicitly selected transition target or effect through the ordinary informed constructor.
- `selection.rs` admits captured projection requests, settles their complete role and evidence standings, and admits the support relationship before recipe construction.
- `admit.rs` admits authored vocabulary, relation, codec, and member rosters into keyed collections and names the first refusal when they do not fit.
- `contracts.rs` holds the cross-clause contracts a recipe must satisfy before projection: evidence targets, the transition account, and every projection's subject and dependency.
- `collisions.rs` finds the generated names the standard projections would occupy and refuses the first one the authored module or another projection already owns.
- `settle.rs` settles declared structural postures against computed relation standings and names the relation refusals the roads above raise.
- `restore.rs` restores caller-authored spans onto the generated material that repeats them.

Capture reaches private construction operations through the recipe declarations; rendering reaches the informed recipe they publish.
Selection requests each deferred syntax reading at its admitted role or table position, while the capture lens retains grammar and token inspection.
Authored namespace and record observations enter their account-owned relationship checks at the existing reading points rather than being deferred until final construction.
Semantic edits re-inform explicit structural parts while retaining the original authored declaration as source material; they do not rebuild those parts by reparsing that declaration.
