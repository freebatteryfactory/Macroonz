# capture

This home reads the one inline-module recipe grammar into the structural parts the recipe account informs.

The door owns the module envelope: locating the authored module, its final `bake!` declaration, and the authored items that stay caller Rust.
Each child owns one clause grammar and nothing else: the bake declaration and its transition sugar, codec declarations, exact dispatch and relation-table signatures, evidence blocks, the authored module's records and enums, projection selection, and generic vocabularies, relations, and postures.

Clause readers produce parts, and the read door composes the declarations' private construction operations into the final informed recipe.
Every structural question those parts raise is answered by that nucleus, and every refusal a reader can name lives in the issue home.
Deferred exact signatures and consuming configuration are interpreted only when account selection reaches their role, and each relation table's subject is admitted before its signature is read.
The namespace and record lenses report authored syntax without owning generated-name or codec-owner policy.
