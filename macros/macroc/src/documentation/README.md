# `documentation` — rustdoc material, and the station it answers to

The documentation projection's delivery: the doc-comment attribute run one
declared item carries, plus the explanation answers the projection's plans owe
the station every kind answers to.

## Two deliveries, and the matrix says so

The delivery matrix spells this projection's delivery as **rustdoc and the
explanation station**, which is two addresses rather than one prose blob written
twice. The rustdoc half is tokens the caller splices ahead of the owner's own
item; the station half is typed
[`ExplanationAnswer`](crate::explanation_protocol::ExplanationAnswer) values
riding the landed
explanation vocabulary. Neither is a rendering of the other, and neither is
prose this home invented.

## Never invented prose

Every rendered sentence traces to exactly one of two sources, and there is no
third:

- **the owner's own doc text**, carried in as [`AuthoredLine`] and
  [`PlainSentence`] and written out unchanged;
- **one typed fact**, rendered from typed values by a closed roster of five
  ([`DocumentedFact`]) — the kind's declared name, the causing declaration, the
  planned output identity, one cited owner fact, and one covered facet.

A [`SectionLine`] is one or the other, so a line that traces to neither is not a
value anybody can hold. That is the whole of what "never invented prose" means
here: it is a shape, not a review.

## The one plain sentence, and what makes a section earned

The summary is ONE PLAIN SENTENCE and the door enforces every word of that: not
empty, not carrying a line break, ending in a full stop, and inside the declared
magnitude. A summary that ran to a paragraph would be the first thing every
reader of the rendered item sees and the last thing anybody could skim.

Sections are EARNED by the plan's own facet roster, and the roster is the
quantifier in both directions: a facet the plan covers with no section written
for it is refused, a section naming a facet the plan does not cover is refused,
and two sections for one facet are refused. All three co-establish freely, so a
caller reads the whole coverage disagreement at once rather than one section per
attempt.

## What this home cannot spell, and says so

The machine's [`Facet`](threadpak::declaration::Facet) roster declares no stable
name and no described
projection — it is a plain six-arm enum. This home therefore cannot write a
facet's NAME into a rendered sentence without inventing one, and inventing one
would be these services legislating a spelling inside a vocabulary the machine
owns. So the facet fact refuses, naming the facet, and the seat that closes it is
a declared name on the machine's own roster.

The coverage law is untouched by that gap, and deliberately: it matches facets by
IDENTITY and never by spelling, so which sections a plan earns is decided without
anybody naming a facet. Only the optional fact that says "this section covers
WHEN" needs a spelling, and only that fact refuses.

The section HEADING is the owner's own authored line for the same reason: a
heading this home composed would be a sentence tracing to neither of the two
admitted sources.

## The item itself is not rendered here

What this home writes is the doc material an item carries — an attribute run —
and the item is the owner's. A documentation projection that emitted the item
would be a second declaration of it, and the plan declares one member landing at
the declaration site, not two.

## The station half

The projection kind lists no questions of its own, so its plans answer exactly
the universal roster and nothing narrower. `plan.rs` composes those answers from
two places and no third: the PLAN, for the declaration it was caused by, the
graph and profile it was decided under, the member it declared, and what
invalidates it; and [`DocumentationExplanationAnchors`], for the four facts a
plan does not hold — the kind's own identity, the owner that required it, the
digest the closure proved over bytes that exist, and the related kind's
disposition beside the repairs a refusal offers. A road that minted any of those
four would be answering the station with a value nobody computed.

## The seats

`types.rs` declares, including the four magnitude rows this home's capacities are
governed by — meaning, number, and reason on one row, stamped through the plane's
`limits!` — and the two refusal families it answers through. Its own child
`type_guard.rs` holds every road that reaches a private seat — a sentence's text,
a line's text, a section's lines, an item's sections, the surface's composition,
and the refusal body's one seat — which is what makes the one-plain-sentence law
a shape rather than a rule. `type_contract.rs` states the declarative tables: the
refusal family's declared shape, and the fact roster with what each arm traces to.
`plan.rs` reads the plan through its public surface and composes the station's
answers. `render.rs` is the token half: the summary attribute, the earned
sections, and the one sentence per typed fact.
