The same battery refuses a planted handwritten road carrying one trailing byte as `FailureClass::PropertyDisagreement` under the owner-declared `shape-subject/generated-versus-planted-wrong-codec` cause.
Both passing and planted-wrong readings retain and externally expose the complete two-row shared-substrate roster.

The generated decoder separately refuses:

- trailing bytes;
- malformed optional presence;
- an unadmitted closed-choice slot;
- a nested member refusal;

- invalid UTF-8;
- a count beyond the declared width;
- a frame length beyond the remaining bytes;
