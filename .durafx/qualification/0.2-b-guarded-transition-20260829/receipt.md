## Two distinct declaration families

The transition family declares:

- the closed state roster `Idle`, `Armed`, and `Done`;
- the closed event roster `Arm` and `Finish`;
- initial standing `Idle` and terminal standing `Done`;
- lawful edges `Idle + Arm -> Armed` and `Armed + Finish -> Done`;
- one subject-owned effect seat and checked numeric effect for each lawful edge.

The policy family separately declares:

- the closed capability roster `Basic` and `Elevated`;
- the closed operation roster `ArmOp` and `FinishOp`;
- associations `Arm => ArmOp` and `Finish => FinishOp`;
- admitted edges `Basic => ArmOp`, `Elevated => ArmOp`, and `Elevated => FinishOp`.

Their only semantic join is ordinary subject execution.
An event must first name an associated operation, the capability-policy edge must admit that operation, and the current standing must independently admit the event transition.
`Basic` therefore refuses the lawful two-event transition sequence at `FinishOp`, while `Elevated + Finish` from `Idle` refuses as an illegal transition even though the policy edge is admitted.
